# VerbaFlo Engineering Manager Hiring Analysis

Date: July 9, 2026

## Executive Summary

This role is realistic only if the company is actually hiring a hands-on backend/AI platform leader, not a conventional people manager.

The job title says **Engineering Manager**, but the JD asks for someone who can personally design and build scalable backend systems, APIs, data pipelines, AI/LLM infrastructure, RAG pipelines, vector databases, LLM serving, inference optimization, CI/CD, production monitoring, architecture, and mentoring. That is a hybrid of:

- Staff/Principal Backend Engineer
- AI Platform Architect
- Tech Lead Manager
- Early-stage startup Engineering Manager

In the current Indian market, this is not a normal EM search. It is a premium AI/backend infrastructure search with a management wrapper.

For Gurugram onsite, 12 pm to 8 pm IST, 9-13 years experience, startup background, and production LLM/RAG exposure, a realistic budget is:

| Candidate Quality | Likely CTC Range |
|---|---:|
| Backend EM with limited real AI infra, mostly API integrations | Rs 45-60 LPA |
| Strong hands-on backend architect with SaaS/startup experience and some RAG/vector DB production exposure | Rs 60-85 LPA |
| True hands-on AI platform/backend leader with production LLM infra, architecture ownership, team mentoring, and startup 0-1/1-10 experience | Rs 85 LPA-1.25 Cr |
| Exceptional candidate from top product/GCC/startup with strong AI infra depth | Rs 1.25-1.6 Cr+ |

My practical recommendation: **do not take this mandate seriously below Rs 70-80 LPA CTC unless they are flexible on AI infra depth, startup stage, onsite requirement, or title.** For a strong close, anchor the founder around **Rs 90 LPA to 1.1 Cr CTC**, with ESOPs as upside, and use Rs 65-75 LPA only as the lower bound for slightly imperfect but still capable candidates.

## Business Analysis: VerbaFlo

VerbaFlo positions itself as an AI communications platform for real estate operators. The product appears to automate conversations across voice, chat, email, SMS/WhatsApp, and other messaging channels. Its use cases include lead acquisition, customer support, debt collection, feedback collection, check-in assistance, lead reactivation, sales enablement, and customer success.

The business is not just a chatbot wrapper. Based on the company site and funding coverage, the product likely needs:

- Multi-channel conversation orchestration across voice, chat, WhatsApp/SMS, and email
- Real estate domain-specific workflows for leasing, resident support, collections, tour booking, and lead qualification
- CRM/property-management integrations
- Knowledge-base/RAG systems for property-specific answers
- Human handover flows
- Conversation analytics and operational dashboards
- Reliability, observability, and compliance for customer-facing automated communication

This explains why the founder may be asking for a heavy backend/AI infrastructure profile. If their product promise is real-time, always-on, multi-channel AI communication, the backend platform becomes the company’s core asset.

## What The Founder Is Probably Looking For

The founder is likely not looking for a classic Engineering Manager who mostly handles people management, sprint planning, 1:1s, hiring, delivery governance, and stakeholder management.

They probably want a **senior technical owner** who can:

- Take backend architecture ownership away from the founder/current senior engineers
- Stabilize and scale production systems after funding
- Make AI features reliable enough for enterprise customers
- Convert ambiguous product requests into technical plans
- Mentor the current team without becoming fully non-hands-on
- Build engineering process without slowing down startup execution
- Represent engineering maturity to investors, customers, and internal teams

In simpler words: they want someone who can be the **backend/AI platform adult in the room** while still shipping code.

## Role Realism

The role is realistic, but only if expectations are clarified.

### Realistic Version

This is realistic if framed as:

**Engineering Manager / Tech Lead Manager - Backend & AI Platform**

Expected split:

- 50-70% hands-on architecture, reviews, critical coding, debugging, platform design
- 20-30% technical planning and cross-functional execution
- 10-20% mentoring, hiring, process, and people leadership

### Unrealistic Version

This becomes unrealistic if the founder expects one person to be:

- Full-time people manager
- Principal backend architect
- AI/LLM infra expert
- DevOps/SRE owner
- Data pipeline owner
- Security owner
- Hands-on coder
- Delivery manager
- Product translator
- Mentor for the whole team

That is too much for one hire unless the company is explicitly hiring a very senior startup engineering leader and paying accordingly.

## Title Mismatch

The current title may hurt sourcing.

Many true Engineering Managers with 9-13 years experience are no longer deeply hands-on in LLM infra, vector databases, RAG, inference optimization, backend architecture, and production debugging. Meanwhile, the people who are strong at those things may not identify as EMs.

Better title options:

- Engineering Manager - Backend & AI Platform
- Tech Lead Manager - AI Platform
- Principal Backend Engineer - AI Platform
- Backend Engineering Lead
- Staff Backend Engineer / Engineering Lead

If the company needs management plus hands-on architecture, “Tech Lead Manager” is probably the most honest title.

## Market Compensation View

Public compensation data supports a wide range. Levels.fyi lists Software Engineering Manager compensation in India with median total compensation around Rs 84.97 LPA, 25th percentile around Rs 58.55 LPA, 75th percentile around Rs 1.25 Cr, and 90th percentile around Rs 1.56 Cr, last updated July 8, 2026. Top-paying companies in India are much higher, but those numbers include large product companies and equity-heavy packages.

The VerbaFlo JD adds a premium because it asks for:

- Backend architecture depth
- Production AI/LLM infrastructure
- RAG/vector DB experience
- Startup Seed/Series A/B experience
- 9-13 years total experience
- Prior EM experience
- Onsite Gurugram
- 12 pm to 8 pm shift

The AI premium matters. Mid-career AI engineering roles in India are already reported in the Rs 30-40 LPA band, and recruiters believe actual AI compensation can be materially higher. For a 9-13 year leader who is expected to own production AI systems, the market clears much higher than a generic backend manager.

## Suggested Budget Position

### Minimum Viable Budget

Rs 65-75 LPA CTC

This can work only if the company accepts trade-offs:

- Candidate may have strong backend and leadership but lighter AI infra
- Candidate may know RAG/vector DB but not serious inference optimization
- Candidate may be more tech lead than proven EM
- Candidate may need ramp-up on voice/telephony/real-time AI systems

### Strong Closing Budget

Rs 85 LPA-1.1 Cr CTC

This is the practical band for a strong candidate who can actually satisfy the JD.

### Premium Budget

Rs 1.1-1.4 Cr CTC plus ESOPs

This is where they can attract sharper candidates from product startups, AI companies, GCCs, or senior platform teams.

### Red Flag Budget

Below Rs 55-60 LPA

At this level, the company should rewrite the role. They are unlikely to close someone who is simultaneously an EM, backend architect, AI infra operator, and startup builder.

## Questions To Ask The Founder

### Role Clarity

1. Is this person expected to code weekly, or only review designs and guide the team?
2. What percentage of the role is hands-on coding versus people management?
3. How many engineers will report to this person on day one?
4. Will this person own hiring, appraisals, 1:1s, and performance management?
5. Is the founder looking for an EM, a tech lead, a staff engineer, or a future Head of Engineering?
6. What are the first 90-day outcomes expected from this hire?
7. What problem made this hire urgent now?
8. Who currently owns backend architecture?
9. What technical decisions is the founder expecting this person to take over?
10. Is this a replacement hire, new role, or post-funding scale-up role?

### Product And Platform

1. What are VerbaFlo’s most critical product workflows today?
2. Which channels are live in production: voice, WhatsApp, SMS, web chat, email?
3. What are the highest-volume workflows: lead qualification, support, collections, tour booking, feedback, or reactivation?
4. What does “AI infrastructure” mean internally: RAG, agents, fine-tuning, LLM routing, inference hosting, observability, or all of these?
5. Are they using hosted LLM APIs, self-hosted models, fine-tuned models, or a mix?
6. What vector database is currently used?
7. What retrieval architecture exists today?
8. Do they have evaluation frameworks for AI responses?
9. How do they measure hallucination, wrong answers, tone issues, and failed handovers?
10. What is the current latency expectation for voice and chat responses?

### Backend Architecture

1. What is the current backend stack: Node.js, Python, Go, or mixed?
2. Is the system monolith, modular monolith, microservices, or event-driven?
3. What databases are used in production: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Elasticsearch?
4. Are queues/event systems already in place: SQS, Celery, Kafka, RabbitMQ, BullMQ?
5. What parts of the backend are currently painful?
6. Where are the biggest scaling bottlenecks?
7. What production incidents have happened in the last 3-6 months?
8. What are the current uptime/SLA expectations?
9. What is the current deployment process?
10. What CI/CD, test coverage, and monitoring are already in place?

### AI/LLM Depth

1. Does the role require building LLM infrastructure or only integrating LLM APIs?
2. What does “not limited to API integrations” mean in practical terms?
3. Are they optimizing inference cost, latency, quality, or reliability?
4. Do they use multi-LLM routing?
5. Do they use prompt versioning and prompt evaluation?
6. Do they have AI observability tools in place?
7. Are agents already live in production?
8. How deterministic do workflows need to be?
9. What happens when AI fails or confidence is low?
10. What level of AI safety, auditability, and compliance is required for customers?

### Team And Org

1. Current engineering team size?
2. Current AI/ML team size?
3. Current frontend/backend/devops split?
4. Is there a dedicated DevOps/SRE person?
5. Is there a dedicated data engineer or ML engineer?
6. Who owns product requirements?
7. How mature are PR reviews, design docs, and release processes?
8. What is the founder’s involvement in technical decisions today?
9. Will this person be allowed to change process and architecture?
10. What seniority gaps exist in the current team?

### Hiring Calibration

1. Which requirement is non-negotiable: EM experience, AI infra, backend architecture, startup experience, or onsite availability?
2. Can the candidate be a Staff/Principal Engineer without formal EM title?
3. Can the candidate have strong AI/backend leadership but no direct report management?
4. Can the candidate be from a GCC/product company if they have startup-like ownership?
5. Is remote/hybrid possible for exceptional candidates?
6. Is relocation support available?
7. Is notice-period buyout available?
8. Are ESOPs meaningful and documented?
9. What is the maximum CTC for a perfect candidate?
10. What is the realistic closing budget after internal approvals?

## Candidate Screening Questions

Use these to separate real candidates from resume-keyword candidates.

### Architecture

1. Describe the most complex backend system you owned end to end.
2. What were the scale, traffic, latency, and reliability requirements?
3. What architecture trade-offs did you make and what would you change now?
4. How do you design APIs for multi-tenant SaaS?
5. How do you handle background jobs, retries, dead-letter queues, and idempotency?

### AI/RAG

1. Explain a RAG pipeline you built or operated in production.
2. Which embedding model and vector DB did you use, and why?
3. How did you chunk, index, retrieve, rank, and evaluate results?
4. How did you detect hallucinations or bad retrieval?
5. What metrics did you track for answer quality, latency, and cost?
6. Have you built or operated agentic workflows? What failed in production?
7. Have you used multi-LLM routing or fallback logic?
8. What is your view on fine-tuning versus RAG versus prompt engineering?

### Production And Reliability

1. Tell me about a serious production incident you handled.
2. What monitoring and alerting did you set up?
3. How did you improve system reliability after the incident?
4. How do you design for graceful degradation when an LLM provider is slow or down?
5. How do you control LLM cost in production?

### Leadership

1. How many engineers have you directly managed?
2. How do you balance coding with management?
3. How do you mentor junior engineers without becoming a bottleneck?
4. How do you handle disagreement with a founder or product leader?
5. How do you convert vague product requirements into engineering execution?

## Negotiation Strategy With The Founder

### Start With Role Reality

Tell the founder:

“The JD is not a standard Engineering Manager role. This is a hands-on backend and AI platform leadership role. The market will price it closer to Staff/Principal Engineer plus EM, not a generic manager.”

### Push For A Real Budget Number

Ask:

“For a candidate who checks 80-90% of this JD, are you prepared to go up to Rs 90 LPA-1.1 Cr CTC? If not, which requirements should we relax first?”

### Use Trade-Offs

If budget is tight, suggest relaxing one or more:

- Prior EM title
- Production LLM infra depth
- Startup Seed/Series A/B requirement
- Onsite 5 days
- 12 pm to 8 pm shift
- Exact database/tooling match
- 9-13 year band

### Reframe The Search

Recommend two search tracks:

1. **Tech Lead Manager / Backend AI Platform Lead**
   - Hands-on, architecture-heavy, can mentor
   - Best fit for the JD

2. **Engineering Manager**
   - People/process/delivery-heavy
   - May need a separate Staff AI/backend engineer underneath

This framing will help the founder decide whether they want one expensive hybrid hire or two more focused hires.

## Search Risk

This search will be difficult because the JD stacks many rare filters:

- 9-13 years experience
- Prior EM experience
- Startup background
- Hands-on backend architecture
- Production AI/LLM infra
- RAG/vector DB/agentic workflow exposure
- Cloud, containers, CI/CD, tests
- Onsite Gurugram
- Mid-day to evening shift

The smallest realistic talent pool will be candidates from AI SaaS startups, conversational AI companies, property-tech SaaS, fintech support automation, B2B SaaS platform teams, and some product/GCC AI platform teams.

## Final Recommendation

Before sourcing, align the founder on these three points:

1. **Is this a hands-on architecture role or a people-manager role?**
2. **What is the true maximum budget for an excellent candidate?**
3. **Which three requirements are genuinely non-negotiable?**

If they want the JD exactly as written, push for **Rs 85 LPA-1.25 Cr CTC** as the realistic closing range. If their budget is below **Rs 60 LPA**, recommend rewriting the role as a Senior Backend Engineer / Backend Lead with AI exposure, not Engineering Manager.
